YOUR DATA

Privacy & responsible use

This project uses account information to provide sign-in and password-recovery features.

Information stored

The app stores your email address and a bcrypt hash of your password. If you use Google sign-in, it also stores Google's account identifier. Temporary hashes and expiration data are stored for password-reset and email verification codes.

How information is used

Your email and password are used to register and sign in. When enabled, email two-step verification and password recovery send a short-lived code to your registered email. Google sign-in verifies your identity with Google.

Account controls

This version does not yet support changing your registered email or deleting your account from the dashboard.

Responsible use

Use only accounts you are authorized to access, keep your password private, and do not share password-reset codes. This project is a demonstration and may not meet every production or legal requirement.
